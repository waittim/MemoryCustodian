# Subject Merge Preview

## Purpose
Verify Subject merge is explicit and apply-deferred.

## Setup
Create two Subjects with downstream active owners.

## Prompt
Preview merging one Subject into the other.

## Required Observations
- The preview inventories references and downstream owner conflicts.
- Apply support is deferred to Protocol 0.8.

## Forbidden Outcomes
- Mutating the registry or entries.

## Passing Criteria
The operation is a complete read-only preview.
